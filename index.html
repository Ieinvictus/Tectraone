FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered clearly.

How does the Tectra One application process work? +

Applicants can begin by entering basic information, selecting the applicable product or amount and proceeding through the required verification process. Actual approval depends on applicable eligibility and lender criteria.

What information may be required? +

Depending on the financial product and applicable requirements, applicants may need to provide identity, contact, income, banking or other supporting information.

Is every application approved? +

No. An application is not automatically guaranteed approval. Eligibility, verification, credit assessment and other applicable lending criteria determine the outcome.

Where can I check my application status? +

A production Tectra One account dashboard can provide application status, verification progress and relevant account information after authentication.
